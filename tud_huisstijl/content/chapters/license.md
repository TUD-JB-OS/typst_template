## TU Delft corporate identity approval

**This version of the template has been reviewed and approved by the TU Delft corporate identity (*huisstijl*) team.** The approval applies specifically to the original, approved version and its prescribed TU Delft branding implementation.

The approval confirms that this version follows the applicable TU Delft visual identity requirements. It does not constitute a general approval of future modifications, alternative designs, or derivative versions.

Users are free to modify and redistribute the template under the CC BY 4.0 license. However, **any modified or derivative version automatically loses the TU Delft corporate identity approval associated with the original version**.

A derivative version must therefore not be presented as reviewed, endorsed, or approved by the TU Delft *huisstijl* team unless it has undergone a separate review and received explicit approval.

## Use by other institutions

The template is intended to be reusable beyond TU Delft. Other universities, research institutions, and organisations are welcome to adopt the publishing workflow and adapt the template to their own corporate identities.

When adapting the template for another institution, users should replace TU Delft-specific branding elements, including logos, colours, and other institutional identifiers, with their own.

The CC BY 4.0 license permits reuse of the licensed material but does not grant permission to use TU Delft trademarks or imply institutional endorsement. Any use of the TU Delft name, logo, or other protected identity elements remains subject to the university's applicable requirements.

## Attribution and derivative works

When distributing an adapted version, users should:

- Credit the original TU Delft MyST–Typst template and its creators.
- Include a reference to the CC BY 4.0 license.
- Clearly indicate that the template has been modified.
- Remove any statement suggesting that the modified version retains the original TU Delft corporate identity approval.
- Obtain separate permission or approval if they wish to claim TU Delft branding compliance for their modified version.

The distinction is important: **the template is openly reusable, but the TU Delft branding approval is specific to the reviewed version and is not transferable to derivative works.**

This approach supports open academic publishing and encourages further development of the template while protecting the integrity of the TU Delft corporate identity and its approval process.
